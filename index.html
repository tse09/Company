Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply for this internship?

Any graduate or working professional looking to transition into HR can apply. No prior HR experience required!

Is the internship online or offline?

Flexible! You can choose hybrid or online based on your convenience. Most mentoring sessions are online 1-on-1.

What's the stipend? Does it increase over time?

Starting stipend is ₹15,000/month. It increases to ₹18,000 from month 4 based on performance!

What's the job placement guarantee?

100% placement guarantee. If not placed within 30 days of completion, we provide additional support at no extra cost.
